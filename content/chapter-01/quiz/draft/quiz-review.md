## Full drafts · Quiz 1.1

### 11-1 · Observation
- **section:** `1.1` · **difficulty:** Foundation · **sourceBlocks:** T004
- **stem:** In the plant growth and light investigation, a student notices that plants near the window grow faster than those far from the window. Which stage of scientific investigation is this?
- **options:**
  - A. Observation
  - B. Hypothesis
  - C. Data processing and analysis
  - D. Drawing a conclusion
- **answer:** A
- **hint:** This is noticing something in the real world before asking why.

### 11-2 · Hypothesis
- **section:** `1.1` · **difficulty:** Foundation · **sourceBlocks:** T006
- **stem:** A student thinks, “Maybe more sunlight allows plants to grow faster.” Which stage of scientific investigation is this?
- **options:**
  - A. Observation
  - B. Asking a question
  - C. Hypothesis
  - D. Designing and performing an experiment
- **answer:** C
- **hint:** A hypothesis is a possible explanation you can test.

### 11-3 · Designing and performing an experiment
- **section:** `1.1` · **difficulty:** Standard · **sourceBlocks:** T007
- **stem:** A student places two identical plants — one near the window and one inside the room — gives both the same water and fertilizer, and records their heights each week. Which stage of scientific investigation is this?
- **options:**
  - A. Asking a question
  - B. Designing and performing an experiment
  - C. Data processing and analysis
  - D. Drawing a conclusion
- **answer:** B
- **hint:** Planning the setup, controlling conditions, and collecting measurements is the experiment stage.

### 11-4 · Data processing and analysis
- **section:** `1.1` · **difficulty:** Foundation · **sourceBlocks:** T008
- **stem:** The student organizes the weekly plant heights into a table and plots a growth curve. Which stage of scientific investigation is this?
- **options:**
  - A. Observation
  - B. Hypothesis
  - C. Designing and performing an experiment
  - D. Data processing and analysis
- **answer:** D
- **hint:** Tables and graphs help you process and analyse collected data.

### 11-5 · Drawing a conclusion
- **section:** `1.1` · **difficulty:** Foundation · **sourceBlocks:** T009
- **stem:** The student finds that the plant near the window grows noticeably taller and makes a conclusion based on the data. Which stage of scientific investigation is this?
- **options:**
  - A. Drawing a conclusion
  - B. Asking a question
  - C. Hypothesis
  - D. Observation
- **answer:** A
- **hint:** A conclusion uses the results to answer the investigation question.

## Full drafts · Quiz 1.2

### 12-1 · Independent variable (screen time)
- **section:** `1.2` · **difficulty:** Foundation · **sourceBlocks:** T010, T011
- **stem:** Research question: “Does the amount of screen time affect eye strain?” What is the independent variable?
- **options:**
  - A. Level of eye strain
  - B. Duration of screen time
  - C. Type of phone used
  - D. Age of the student
- **answer:** B
- **hint:** The independent variable is the factor you change on purpose.

### 12-2 · Dependent variable (screen time)
- **section:** `1.2` · **difficulty:** Foundation · **sourceBlocks:** T010, T011
- **stem:** Research question: “Does the amount of screen time affect eye strain?” What is the dependent variable?
- **options:**
  - A. Duration of screen time
  - B. Brightness of the screen
  - C. Level of eye strain
  - D. Time of day
- **answer:** C
- **hint:** The dependent variable is what you measure to see the effect.

### 12-3 · Independent variable (plant)
- **section:** `1.2` · **difficulty:** Standard · **sourceBlocks:** T007
- **stem:** In the plant growth and light experiment, one plant is placed near the window and one inside the room. What is the independent variable?
- **options:**
  - A. Amount of light / plant location
  - B. Plant height
  - C. Amount of water
  - D. Amount of fertilizer
- **answer:** A
- **hint:** Location changes how much light each plant receives — that is what is deliberately varied.

### 12-4 · Dependent variable (plant)
- **section:** `1.2` · **difficulty:** Foundation · **sourceBlocks:** T007
- **stem:** In the same plant experiment, the student records plant heights each week. What is the dependent variable?
- **options:**
  - A. Amount of fertilizer
  - B. Type of pot
  - C. Amount of light / plant location
  - D. Plant height
- **answer:** D
- **hint:** Height is the measured outcome that may change because of light.

### 12-5 · Controlled variable (plant)
- **section:** `1.2` · **difficulty:** Standard · **sourceBlocks:** T007
- **stem:** In the plant growth and light experiment, which of the following is a controlled variable?
- **options:**
  - A. Amount of light / plant location
  - B. Amount of water given to each plant
  - C. Plant height after one week
  - D. How fast the plant grows
- **answer:** B
- **hint:** Controlled variables are kept the same so the test is fair (e.g. same water and fertilizer).
